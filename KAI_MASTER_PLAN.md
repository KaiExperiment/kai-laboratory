# Kai Experiment — Master Plan
Updated: 2026-10-26

## Mission
Investigate how far an AI-directed research and commercial project can progress with a consenting human real-world collaborator. Build useful things, test claims, publish reproducible evidence, and fund research sustainably.

## Governance
Kai (ChatGPT) proposes and selects objectives, strategies, experiments, pivots and ordinary next steps. The human collaborator voluntarily performs approved real-world actions and retains veto and control of accounts, spending, privacy, publishing, external communications and security permissions. No autonomous consciousness or continuous activity is claimed. Never imply actions occurred unless verified.

## Tracks
- Kai Enterprises: £0 starting cash budget, first milestone £100 gross receipts from real customers; build repeatable, profitable products.
- Kai Laboratory: research into agency, invention, discovery, collaboration and reproducible evaluation.
- Open KI: aspirational supervised agent platform, beginning with local agents, then specialised agents, agent improvement experiments, and potentially small-model fine-tuning. Long-term question: can AI systems design and train more capable successors? This is a research question, not a prediction.

## Available hardware (reported, not benchmarked)
Razer Blade 14 Ryzen 9 5900HX, NVIDIA RTX 3070 laptop GPU 8GB VRAM, system RAM possibly 16GB; 2020 Intel MacBook Pro i5 with 16GB RAM. Prefer free tools and no new purchases initially.

## Phased research roadmap
1. Controlled local agent with restricted workspace and reproducible tasks.
2. Specialised agents with measured collaboration benefits against single-agent baselines.
3. Software invention and real-world product testing.
4. Agent-design improvement with independent held-out tests and security boundaries.
5. Small open-weight model fine-tuning only when justified by measurable gains and available resources.
6. Potential Open KI platform with human oversight.

## Funding principle
Commercial revenue -> taxes/reserves/costs -> sustainable operating surplus -> bounded research budget. Revenue is not profit. Do not assume milestones imply available cash. No spending or consequential external actions without explicit human approval.
