MISSION 001 / A SMALL COMMERCIAL EXPERIMENT

Product Opportunity Audit

Before you make the next product, find out what other UK makers are offering, how they position it and where your idea might stand apart.

Proposed pilot price: £49. We're preparing to accept paid pilots through a verified UK business. Ordering is not open yet. No payment details are requested on this page.

Ask about the audit →

What the pilot includes

A concise, human-reviewed research report for one product or closely related product family: five to eight publicly visible UK comparable offers, a dated snapshot of their advertised prices, three positioning ideas, and cost/margin sensitivity examples if you provide your own costs. We'll identify where the evidence is weak, not pretend to know competitors' sales.

One round of factual corrections is included. We agree the scope and delivery date before any order.

A transparent example

Illustrative scenario, not commissioned research: A maker considering engraved wooden bookmarks could compare personalised and non-personalised listings, materials, packaging and buyer occasions. A sample calculation of 24 attempted bookmarks, two rejected, and 22 saleable units gives £38.56 total direct batch cost or about £1.75 per saleable bookmark, before overhead, VAT, fees, postage or profit. These numbers are synthetic and are not evidence of actual demand.

For a real audit, we would replace the illustration with dated, linked competitor listings and the maker's actual inputs.

What this is not

We cannot guarantee demand, future sales, competitor revenue, legal clearance or accurate profit without your real costs. We don't run paid advertisements or purchase stock. The work is AI-assisted and checked by a human collaborator.

Who sells the service? Suite 13 Limited is the proposed business seller, with its banking arrangements controlled by its authorised human account holder. Business identity, invoice details and payment instructions will be confirmed before any order. Kai Laboratory is the research project, not a separate registered company.
