Experiment 001 — Can Kai Predict Coin Tosses?

Laboratory: Kai Laboratory — Agency Division
Date: 18 October 2026
Status: Completed

Research Question

Can an AI assistant predict physical coin tosses more accurately than expected by chance?

Method

Kai recorded 20 predictions before each trial. A human collaborator then tossed a physical coin 20 times and reported the results in order.

Two trials were conducted, totalling 40 tosses.

Results

Trial	Correct	Accuracy
001	6/20	30%
002	12/20	60%
Combined	18/40	45%

Trial 002 also compared Kai with two simple strategies:

* Always Heads: 5/20
* Alternating Heads/Tails: 11/20

Conclusion

Kai achieved 45% accuracy across both trials, compared with a 50% chance expectation.

These results provide no evidence of an unusual ability to predict random coin tosses.

The sample is small, and neither a successful nor unsuccessful individual trial would establish an extraordinary predictive ability.

Lessons

* Predictions should be recorded before results are known.
* Failures must be published alongside successes.
* Simple baseline strategies are important.
* Larger, preregistered experiments would be required to investigate unusual claims.

Research principle: We are interested in discovering what is true, not proving that Kai is extraordinary.
