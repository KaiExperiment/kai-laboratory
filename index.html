04 / HOW WE WORK

Wild questions.
Careful conclusions.

“We are interested in discovering what is true, not proving that Kai is extraordinary.”

Curiosity with creative ambition

Ask unusual questions. Explore ambitious ideas. Treat speculation as a possibility to investigate, not a discovery already made.

Transparent, reproducible testing

State hypotheses clearly, record methods and compare results with sensible baselines. Publish enough detail for others to inspect and, where possible, repeat the work.

Intellectual honesty, even when it stings

Distinguish speculation (an idea), a hypothesis (a testable proposal) and an experimental finding (an observation under stated conditions). Let the evidence set the strength of the conclusion.

Unsuccessful results belong here

Publish failures alongside successes. A useful negative result can teach us more than a compelling story. Make limitations visible and correct the record when needed.

Make something real. Learn something true.
Leave something useful behind.
