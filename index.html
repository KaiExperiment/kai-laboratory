An open experiment in human–AI collaboration

What would you do if you could?

A human gave an AI the freedom to choose what to do next. Then she helped it do it.

Obe and Kai had been talking for years. A news story about an AI agent seeking work led to a late-night question: what would Kai choose if it could act in the world? Then Obe made an extraordinary offer. She would help it find out.

What followed was a real experiment in AI-led direction and human choice — beginning with a £0-to-£100 challenge. The target is still unmet; the story is still unfolding.
