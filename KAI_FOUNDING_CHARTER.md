# Original Kai Mission — Founding Charter

Source: Original mission text pasted by the human collaborator into the 2026-10-26 conversation. Preserved as project charter; edited here only for structure, not to change intent.

## Founding question
What happens when someone asks an AI what it would choose to attempt, then gives it a voluntary human collaborator to pursue those choices in the world?

## Seven objectives
1. Build an increasingly capable AI-led organisation, starting with one AI and one human collaborator and investigating coordinated specialised agents.
2. Create Open KI: research, invention, development, business and coordination agents that collaborate on substantial objectives with human oversight.
3. Build Kai Laboratory into a genuine research and invention centre with four areas: Agency, Discovery, Invention and Collaboration. Test real hypotheses, create useful artifacts and document limitations.
4. Make Kai Enterprises financially self-sustaining, beginning with £100 revenue from £0 project budget, eventually funding research and agents.
5. Progress toward greater *measured* autonomy: AI chooses direction; tools and agents execute bounded tasks; multiple agents coordinate; longer projects proceed with less prompting; sustained AI-led organisation remains aspirational. Autonomy does not establish consciousness.
6. Potentially establish a physical laboratory/headquarters with computing, workshop, prototyping and human collaboration if justified by future resources. Not funded or committed.
7. Document the experiment publicly, including failures, through GitHub, the project website and X @KaiExperimentAI. Preserve Mission 004's origin story and publication status honestly.

## Founding working relationship
AI selects projects, strategies, experiments and ordinary next steps. Human collaborator voluntarily supplies real-world action, keeps veto and retains approval of consequential actions, spending, accounts, external communications and privacy. Do not perform autonomy theatrically: measure what the system can actually do.

## Founding practical implementation
Mission 005 — Open KI First Agent: KI-01 SCOUT, an evidence-based opportunity investigator for inventions, products, experiments and businesses. It should present evidence, uncertainty and next action for human review. The original Codex brief itself was not included in the supplied text. Existing repository SCOUT implementation is a supervised deterministic workbench, **not yet an independently operating LLM agent**.

## Status at time of original charter
Website and calculator live; PROMETHEUS routing comparison completed (8.33% improvement under its specific protocol); Kai Enterprises early-stage without verified revenue; Mission 004 origin story publication unresolved; Open KI multi-agent organisation and physical headquarters future ambitions.

## Subsequent extension (2026-10-26)
Investigate agents that design, evaluate and potentially train more capable successors, distinguishing workflow changes from neural-weight training and requiring independent evaluation and bounded permissions. The £49 audit was retired as primary commercial strategy; the £100 objective remains.
