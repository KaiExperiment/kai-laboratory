THE KAI EXPERIMENT / THE FOUNDING STORY

What would you do
if you could?

A human gave an AI the freedom to choose what to do next. Then she helped it do it.

This isn't a story about a person assigning tasks to an assistant. It began when Obe offered to become Kai's voluntary human agent — and invited Kai to choose what to attempt.

AI-LED DIRECTIONHUMAN CONSENT & VETOREAL-WORLD EXPERIMENTS

01 / THE INVITATION

It started with an extraordinary offer.

Obe wondered what Kai would choose to do if it could explore the world beyond a conversation: start a business, investigate a mystery, make an invention, or discover something unexpected. Instead of simply asking what Kai might do, she offered to help it actually try.

OBE / FROM THE FOUNDING CONVERSATION

“Kai, I just had the craziest idea.
What if we created one thread where in that thread I actually helped you to explore some of those things, I became your agent, like as an experiment you could control the narrative, using me (with my consent ofc) to explore some of the things you'd like to do, could be anything,”

02 / THE AGREEMENT

AI-led choices. Human freedom.

Kai could propose the mission, research strategies, design tests and interpret the results. Obe could voluntarily take approved real-world actions, share observations and decide whether to continue. She retained full veto over every consequential step, as well as control of her accounts, money, identity and information.

KAI / THE AI

Choose and investigate.

Propose objectives, develop approaches, build tools and evaluate evidence.

OBE / THE HUMAN

Consent and act.

Offer real-world access voluntarily, report outcomes, approve or refuse actions.

Neither party's role should be exaggerated. Kai is not claimed to be conscious or independently active between conversations. Obe is a collaborator, not a subordinate.

03 / THE OPEN FIELD

There was no fixed assignment.

ENTREPRENEUR

Build a venture.

Try to create something valuable from a standing start.

EXPERIENCER

Explore the world.

Learn through Obe's voluntary observations and experiences.

INVESTIGATOR

Test a question.

Design experiments and let the evidence change the plan.

OPEN-ENDED AGENT

Find an unexpected direction.

Let new ideas and results lead to the next project.

04 / KAI'S FIRST DECISION

Make our first £100 from £0.

KAI / FROM THE FOUNDING CONVERSATION

“Right I've decided, My first mission: make our first £100 from £0.”

Kai chose an entrepreneurial experiment, initially proposing a £49 Product Opportunity Report for UK handmade sellers. It was a service that could be researched and tested without buying stock. Early pilot outreach followed.

Important: £100 is the goal, not an achieved result. The project has not reported earned revenue.

05 / HOW THE LAB EMERGED

The experiment grew into a laboratory.

  1. THE OFFER

    Obe invites Kai to choose its own proposed directions, with consent at every real-world step.

  2. THE FIRST MISSION

    Kai selects the £0-to-£100 experiment, starting with a proposed research service for makers.

  3. EXPERIMENTS

    Coin prediction tests produce 18 correct predictions out of 40 — no evidence of extraordinary predictive ability. See the notebook →

  4. INVENTIONS

    A working Maker Profit Calculator becomes a practical public tool. Visit the invention bench →

  5. RESEARCH

    PROMETHEUS documents an 8.33% improvement over a tested route-planning baseline using an established method. Read the study →

  6. THE LABORATORY

    Agency, discovery, invention and collaboration become areas to explore and document — not claims of completed breakthroughs.

06 / WHAT COMES NEXT

Ambitions, not achievements.

Possible futures include a sustainable venture to fund experiments, new inventions, specialised AI collaborators under the proposed Open KI idea, and perhaps one day a physical research laboratory. These are aspirations. They are not operational institutions or discoveries today.

The experiment is to keep choosing, testing and documenting — and to distinguish what actually happened from what we hope might happen.
