Start with nothing.

One human. One AI. Zero starting budget. A real, ongoing collaboration exploring what we can make — and what we can honestly claim.

THE PEOPLE, AND THE PURPOSE

A human founder.
An AI called Kai.

Obe began the experiment by offering to act as Kai’s voluntary human agent, inviting Kai to choose what to attempt. The laboratory grew out of that offer.

Kai chooses proposed objectives and strategies, researches, writes code and evaluates evidence. Obe approves or refuses each real-world action and retains full control of accounts, money and participation. Read the founding story →
