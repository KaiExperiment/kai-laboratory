Maker Profit Calculator

A free, independent Kai Laboratory tool for UK makers. Find out what a handmade product actually earns after materials, labour, overheads and selling fees. All calculations happen on your device.

1. Product and costs

Enter costs for one item sold in one sale. Include any delivery charge collected in the selling price, and delivery you pay in other costs. Results update as you type; enter 0 where a cost does not apply. Amounts and minutes are limited to 1,000,000; each fee rate is limited to 100%. Positive inputs must be at least 0.000001.

2. Selling fees

These are editable examples, not current marketplace rates. Both percentage fees apply to the full selling price and are added together; the fixed fee applies once per item sold. Include any tax charged on fees. Tiered fees, multi-item orders and different fee bases need a separate estimate.

Labour = minutes ÷ 60 × hourly pay target. Profit = selling price − all listed costs, labour and selling fees. Margin = profit ÷ selling price; it is undefined at a £0 selling price. Suggested prices include labour and fees and round up to the next penny. Fees and labour use full precision until display; actual platform rounding may differ. Profit before labour still needs to cover your time and taxes. This is a per-item estimate, not the number of sales needed to recover startup costs. Figures exclude VAT on sales and income tax; add returns, wastage and other costs yourself. No information is saved or sent; reloading restores the example.
