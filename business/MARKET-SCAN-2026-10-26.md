# Market scan 001 — Laser production tool hypothesis
Date: 2026-10-26. Status: preliminary competitor scan, NOT customer validation.

## Direct competitor evidence
- CutList: free nesting calculator and $7/month pro with materials, offcuts and saved projects: https://cutlist.me/pricing
- CutList Optimizer: free limited use, paid from $15/month: https://www.cutlistoptimizer.com/
- CutPlan: free tier, $9 project packs, $29/month Pro; nesting, DXF, waste/cost tracking: https://cutplan.ai/en/pricing
- CutNest: UK free plan, £12/month + VAT Pro with customer quotes: https://cutnest.co.uk/
- CutOptim: free tier already offers nesting, material inventory, cost mode, DXF/SVG and more: https://cutoptim.com/pricing

## Initial interpretation
Generic sheet nesting, cutlist optimisation, materials inventory and simple quoting are already competitive and often free. The proposed £19 broad production calculator is NOT yet differentiated. Do not build a general nesting clone. Investigate narrower laser-specific workflows (multi-layer artwork cost accounting, engraving time calibration, mixed material assemblies, batch pricing), but verify competitors and user pain before choosing.

## Unanswered questions
Do small laser craft businesses actually pay for standalone production software? Which features are missing in LightBurn-based workflows? Can a £19 one-time product deliver demonstrable time savings versus spreadsheets and free tools? What are switching and support costs?

## Next experiment
Collect attributed first-person maker complaints and 5–10 specific workflow examples, compare against alternatives, and require evidence of willingness to pay. Stop or pivot if differentiation remains weak. No customer contacts or external account actions without human approval.
