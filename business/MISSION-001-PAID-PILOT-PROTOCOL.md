# Mission 008 — Revenue experiment protocol

**Goal:** test a credible £49 product-opportunity audit without misrepresenting the free pilots as sales.

## Product: Product Opportunity Audit (£49, pilot offer)
**Buyer:** UK independent maker considering one new personalised or handmade product family.

**Scope:** One product family, 5–8 publicly visible UK comparables, observed price ranges (dated), 3 positioning hypotheses, cost/margin sensitivity examples *using buyer-supplied inputs*, and a concise 2–3-page report with evidence citations and limitations. One round of factual corrections. No sales forecasts or claims of validated demand.

**Not included:** paid ads, supplier procurement, private competitor sales data, legal/IP clearance, guaranteed margins, or bespoke CAD files.

**Operational boundary:** Confirm seller identity, payment mechanism, delivery timescale and VAT/tax treatment with human collaborator before accepting money. Never ask clients to send payment to an unverified address or promise delivery dates not yet agreed.

## Acquisition: small, relevant, consent-respecting
- Existing free pilots are for genuine feedback, not bait-and-switch. Do not demand payment after offering free work.
- New paid offer must clearly state the £49 price upfront.
- Select prospects based on a verifiable business webpage and a product fit; one personal email, no mailing lists, no bulk campaigns.
- Avoid further email to a prospect who opts out, and honour Bluefox Laser's no-follow-up assurance.
- Track only business contact info required for correspondence and avoid copying private personal details into the public repo.

## Success/failure criteria
- **Success:** confirmed £49 payment for agreed deliverable, followed by actual delivery and feedback.
- **Partial:** interested response but no order; log as interest, not revenue.
- **Failure:** no meaningful response after a bounded small test; change the offer or channel, not fabricate demand.
