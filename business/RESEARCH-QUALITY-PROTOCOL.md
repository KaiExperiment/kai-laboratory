# Mission 017 — Evidence quality protocol

Date: 20 October 2026. Purpose: make every paid Product Opportunity Audit more reliable than an attractive collection of links.

## Source verification (mandatory before delivery)

For each comparable, record: seller, product name, URL, date checked, advertised base price, currency, VAT wording, delivery charge or unknown, personalisation options, material, size, minimum order quantity and stock/availability status where visible.

1. Open the **actual product page**, not just a search snippet. If unavailable, mark **unverified** and exclude it from numerical conclusions.
2. Record what the seller actually says, with the observation date. Distinguish sale price from normal price and **ex-VAT** from VAT-inclusive or unspecified pricing.
3. Check if shipping, gift packaging, engraving or customisation costs extra. Never describe a price as delivered unless checkout evidence supports that.
4. Compare similar specifications. Where a price range mixes unlike products, state the differences and avoid a misleading average.
5. Separate **observed facts**, **calculations**, **assumptions**, **hypotheses** and **recommendations** in the report.
6. Never infer sales volume, demand, conversion rate, search ranking, revenue or profitability from listings alone.
7. If seller pages disagree with earlier notes, correct the notes, preserve the discrepancy in the change log and flag any downstream conclusion affected.
8. Use only costs supplied or approved by the client for their own margin estimates. Synthetic numbers must be labelled demonstration only.
9. Human reviewer checks links, arithmetic, wording and commercial scope before the report is sent. Never promise a forecast or guaranteed results.
10. Keep customer contact information, bank/payment details and confidential cost figures out of the public repository.

## Reproducible calculations

- Saleable units = attempted units − rejects.
- Direct unit cost = total direct batch costs ÷ saleable units (only if saleable units > 0).
- Indicative unit contribution = customer selling price − direct unit cost − per-unit selling fees − per-unit fulfilment charges.
- **Not net profit:** labour, overhead, tax, returns, postage and other costs must be included or prominently flagged if omitted.
- Show the inputs, units, rounding, and at least three plausible scenarios.

## Quality gates

- **Red:** broken source, unverified advertised price, unlabelled VAT, missing critical client cost input or unsupported demand claim. Do not send as a verified finding.
- **Amber:** valid source but material differences or missing ancillary charges. Include caveats; do not use for like-for-like conclusions.
- **Green:** accessible dated source, comparable specification and disclosed price basis. Still no proof of demand.

## Public sample limitations

The [wooden bookmark demonstration](../sample-wooden-bookmark-audit.html) is an illustrative format, **not** a decision-grade client report. Its remaining preliminary comparables need independent verification. This protocol is the standard for future paid work.
