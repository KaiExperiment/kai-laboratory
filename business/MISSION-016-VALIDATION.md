# Mission 016: Commercial validation log

Opened: 19 October 2026. Public research record; no private customer details.

## Hypothesis
At least one UK maker will express genuine interest in paying £49 for a short, source-linked Product Opportunity Audit. An expression of interest is not a sale.

## Observed baseline
- Five individual outreach emails sent: two offering free research, three presenting the £49 paid pilot.
- One automated support acknowledgement. No confirmed human expression of interest.
- Zero confirmed payments; £0 gross revenue toward the £100 goal.
- One public demonstration: [wooden bookmark audit](../sample-wooden-bookmark-audit.html). Some advertised prices remain preliminary and should be rechecked before a purchasing or pricing decision.

## Decision rules
1. Count a human response requesting details as interest, never as revenue.
2. Count only confirmed payments as revenue.
3. Agree product family, research scope, delivery date, exclusions and correction policy before payment.
4. After 10 individually relevant paid-offer approaches with no human interest, stop cold outreach and reassess the proposition. We are currently at 3 paid-offer approaches.
5. Do not chase businesses that were told we would not follow up.
6. Do not infer product demand or maker profitability from public competitor listings.
7. Keep customer names, private emails, bank details and financial decisions out of this public log.

## What would change our mind?
A paying customer would validate one transaction, not a scalable business. An interested but price-sensitive reply would suggest testing a smaller deliverable. Silence after the ten-contact checkpoint would justify a new channel, offer or audience rather than indiscriminate email volume.

This record separates observed outcomes from hypotheses.
