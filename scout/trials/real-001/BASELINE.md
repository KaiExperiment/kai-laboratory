# Mission 005 — baseline decision memo, first public-evidence trial

Date: 2026-10-19. Prepared by Kai from retrieved public material, before any SCOUT execution on this dataset.

## Candidates
1. Marketplace fee scenario comparator: primary Etsy documentation establishes several kinds of fees. It does not establish customer demand or an unmet need. Existing Maker Profit Calculator may already cover much of this.
2. Materials/stock consumption ledger: prior reconnaissance lists anecdotal Reddit discussion, but its original text was not independently recovered during this pass. Demand, applicability to UK makers, and differentiation are unverified.
3. Batch-cost/price-variation analyser: conceivable local CSV prototype; no verified independent demand evidence yet, and it may overlap with the existing calculator.

## Baseline ranking (judgment, not SCOUT output)
- First research priority: materials/stock consumption ledger, **for further evidence collection only**. Potentially less overlap with the existing calculator, but the demand case is weak.
- Second: batch-cost/price-variation analyser, **for investigation only**.
- Third: marketplace-fee scenario comparator, **for investigation only**, pending an audit of calculator features.

## Baseline decision
Do not build or commercialise any candidate on current evidence. Seek two independent relevant real-world sources per candidate, check existing competitors and calculator overlap, then rerank. No demonstrated willingness to pay.

## Trial integrity
This is an authored baseline, not blinded and not a measured benchmark. The single preserved primary source excerpt is about Etsy fee policy and does not independently support market demand for any candidate. The SCOUT executable has not been run on this trial. Any future result must separately report its actual CLI output, abstention gates and comparison with this memo. Do not claim autonomous discovery.
