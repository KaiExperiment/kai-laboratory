# Mission 007 — competitive reality check (2026-10-19)

**Method:** public webpage feature descriptions reviewed; no interactive browser execution of competitors, no maker interviews, no validated willingness to pay.

## Evidence that challenges our proposed differentiation

| Tool | Publicly described features | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Crafters Vinyl | Quantity, setup time per batch, material wastage %, VAT, platform presets, margin/markup, batch revenue/profit | Setup and wastage alone are **not** novel |
| Indigo & Paper | Batch materials, items made, unsaleable items, hours per batch, direct sales, wholesale, consignment | Explicit rejects plus channel comparisons already advertised |
| CraftBatch recipe cost calculator | Batch ingredients and labour, per-unit yield | Batch-first costing exists for recipe makers |
| CostReady Quick Coster | Batch ingredient costs, sellable units, batch time and fixed batch costs | Sellable yield and fixed batch costs are not novel |
| Huskai | Per-item costs, labour, packaging, overhead, margin and platform fees | Strong free per-item baseline |
| FundiJumla | Failed output, craft-specific calculators, wholesale/quote tools | Broad category coverage |
| CraftBatch paid platform | Recipe, inventory, batch tracking; advertises £15/month future plan | Evidence of vendor price positioning, **not** customer willingness to pay |

## Research decision
**Do not greenlight a paid general-purpose maker pricing calculator.** We have discovered direct substitutes for the core proposed features. This is a provisional **competitive caution**, not a proven lack of demand or proof that every competitor correctly handles T1. Need hands-on benchmark comparisons, real workflow observation, and actual payment evidence before a new build.

## Falsifiable follow-up questions
1. Does a competitor treat rejected units as part of attempted labour and materials while charging packaging only for saleable units?
2. Can a maker complete T1 correctly without manually dividing costs or changing the meaning of quantity fields?
3. What non-pricing task is repeatedly costly for laser-cut makers (quoting custom jobs, managing variants, stock/material nesting, order changes), and what do they currently pay for?
4. Can the existing £49 Product Opportunity Audit earn a paid pilot without building another tool?

**Data integrity:** This file reports only published feature descriptions and the research team's inferences; it is not a user study or a competitive execution test.
