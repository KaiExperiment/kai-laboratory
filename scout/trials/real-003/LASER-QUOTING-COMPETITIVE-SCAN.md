# Mission 008 — Laser quoting competitive reconnaissance

Date: 2026-10-19. **Method: public feature descriptions, not hands-on executions.** Do not claim competitor output accuracy or customer demand.

| Provider | Public claim | Consequence for Kai Laboratory |
|---|---|---|
| CutNest | Free sheet/bar nesting; Pro £12/month or £99/year + VAT with multi-material, grain lock, offcuts, customer quotes and DXF | Generic nesting + quotation is already an occupied category |
| Quote & Cut | DXF uploads, true-shape nesting, machine/material rates, WooCommerce integration, free starter allowance and higher paid tiers | Customer self-service and commercial pricing engines exist |
| Precision Laser Cutting | Public instant DXF quoting portal, automatic nesting, material and quantity selections | Workflow already offered in production |
| CutQuote | Geometry-aware quotes, DXF/DWG/manual shapes, paid true-shape nesting, shop rates | Shape analysis and price estimation are established |
| PrixCut | DXF analysis, nesting previews and priced quote exports on paid plans | DXF-to-quote is not novel |
| OneLaser | Quotes based on cut length, pierces, material; £75 minimum order | Not all operators offer immediate quotes, but manual review can be deliberate |
| Cut It Out | DXF/STEP quoting and online ordering | Another existing provider |

## Decision
**Do not greenlight a generic laser nesting or DXF quoting app.** Competitor breadth is substantial. Published prices are vendor offers, **not evidence of paid conversions**. Features may differ in actual performance; no hands-on benchmark performed.

## Next experiments
1. Test a mixed-material, grain-constrained, variable-quantity small craft order in two tools with a common input; document exact outputs, access limits and errors.
2. Ask makers about specific painful manual handoffs *outside* nesting (customer personalisation proof approvals, revisions, engraving settings, packaging/dispatch).
3. Prototype only if a measurable gap survives those comparisons.
