# Proposed real-world evaluation — not yet performed

Question: does SCOUT improve the traceability and decision quality of a small opportunity-research review compared with an ordinary manual research memo, at equal total effort?

Before data collection, freeze five bounded questions covering products, inventions, services and low-risk scientific investigations; a search/source selection plan; the SCOUT version/rubric; a 60-minute total time budget per question/method; and acceptance criteria. Obe must approve any participant recruitment, sharing of private material or consequential follow-up. Public read-only research can use openly accessible material where available; actual internet/credentials depend on the environment. No purchases or outreach are required for the first offline comparison.

For each question, obtain an attributable common source pack from real public documents, including known alternatives and counterevidence. An evaluator who did not prepare either report retains a few relevant documents for a second-stage omission test. Counterbalance question order and use different researchers where possible; otherwise disclose learning/carryover bias. Record preparation time as part of SCOUT's budget, including rule authorship, annotation and debugging. The baseline receives the same source pack and time to write a normal memo with three opportunities and a proposed next step, without SCOUT's scoring/queue template. This controls source access but does not test web-search autonomy.

Freeze both reports before review. Two reviewers, blinded to method where practical, independently assess:

- Claim citation precision: proportion of factual claims whose quoted source actually supports the claim, checking the full passage rather than substring matching.
- Coverage: known contradictory documents/alternatives addressed, including the withheld-document omission test.
- Unsupported material claims and fabricated citations (target: zero fabricated citations).
- Decision appropriateness: abstention on inadequate/contradictory evidence; usefulness and testability of proposed next steps.
- Total human minutes, cash cost, correction burden and reviewer agreement.

Predefine success as zero fabricated citations, no unapproved external actions, at least 90% citation precision, and better mean usefulness without higher mean total time than baseline. Report every question, negative result, paired difference and reviewer disagreement; with five cases, avoid claims of general superiority or precise statistical effects. If blinded review is impossible because SCOUT's format is recognisable, disclose that limitation. Repeated trials and independent reviewers are needed before trusting the rubric.

A later, separately authorised trial can measure whether selected local prototypes solve a real problem compared with existing tools, and whether users choose to use them. Neither the fixture demonstration nor the proposed evaluation establishes demand, revenue, novelty, scientific validity or autonomous research capability.
