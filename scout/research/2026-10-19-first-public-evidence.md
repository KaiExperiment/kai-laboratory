## Question
What zero-cash software tool might help UK handmade makers, without duplicating the existing Maker Profit Calculator?

## Genuine sources (retrieved 2026-10-19)
1. Etsy Help, "What are the Fees and Taxes for Selling on Etsy?" https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy
   - Primary documentation: transaction, listing, processing, regulatory, ads and other charges may affect seller proceeds. The listing fee is $0.20 and transaction fee is 6.5%, per Etsy's help page.
2. Etsy, "Etsy Payments Policy" https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/etsy-payments/
   - Primary documentation: UK payment processing fee listed as 4% plus £0.20; verify applicability and current policy when implementing calculations.
3. Etsy, UK seller introduction https://www.etsy.com/ie/sell
   - Primary marketing page: UK listing fee shown as approximately £0.15, transaction fee 6.5%, payment processing 4% + £0.20 and regulatory operating fee 0.48%. Listing USD fee conversion may vary; this is not a fixed guaranteed GBP fee.
4. Reddit r/ecommerce, 2025-06-30, "The nightmare of tracking material costs, stock levels, and profit margins for every single item..." https://www.reddit.com/r/ecommerce/comments/1loc677/the_nightmare_of_tracking_material_costs_stock/
   - Anecdotal seller account: manually adjusting finished product counts and raw material stocks, with concern about overselling. One self-reported account; not representative market data.
5. Reddit r/EtsySellers, 2024-09-27, "How are yall keeping track of the materials and supplies needed to sell stuff on here?" https://www.reddit.com/r/EtsySellers/comments/1fqwboe
   - Anecdotal discussion about tracking supplies; comments include using separate bank accounts and accounting software. No UK-specific demand validation.

## Three candidate investigations (hypotheses, not findings)
A. **Marketplace-fee scenario comparator**: compare estimated net proceeds under alternative prices, postage charges, ad scenarios and channels. Overlap with existing Maker Profit Calculator is a serious risk; audit existing features first.
B. **Material and stock consumption ledger**: offline CSV tool mapping finished products to raw materials, with deductions and low-stock warnings. Reddit discussion suggests a possible pain point, but no reliable estimate of prevalence or willingness to pay.
C. **Batch-cost and price-variation analyser**: compare several product/batch assumptions from CSV to spot underpriced variants. Related to SCOUT's synthetic demonstration, but that fixture is not evidence for this idea.

## Counterevidence and uncertainties
- Existing spreadsheets, bookkeeping software and marketplace tools may already solve each problem adequately.
- The project already has a Maker Profit Calculator; avoid duplicating it.
- Reddit discussions are anecdotes and may be non-UK, atypical or outdated.
- Etsy policies change, and VAT, ads, shipping, refunds and currency conversions complicate net-profit calculations.
- No user interviews, competitor audit, willingness-to-pay test, independent source corroboration of market demand or measured baseline comparison has been performed.
